# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-05)

First public release.

- Nine governance controls: C1–C7 core, C8 agent identity and authority, C9 evals and monitoring.
- Evidence ladder (Absent / Partial / Implemented / Proven), with caps by mode (code, doc, interview).
- Required level by context tier (T1 internal, T2 customer-facing or enterprise-sold, T3 regulated or consequential), plus a rule for mixed-tier systems.
- Claim–reality gap detection, bypass hunting, and the nine-question reconstruction test.
- Consequence mapping to the EU AI Act, GDPR, PIPEDA, Quebec Law 25, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, OWASP LLM Top 10 and enterprise questionnaires.
- Engineering tickets: at most 8, with a backlog and a verification list.
- Two test fixtures with an answer key. Both verdicts and all seeded traps matched.
